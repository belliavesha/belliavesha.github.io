The split snaps to the edges of the 15 map bands (0.2–11 keV, log spaced), so Eb takes eight values. A positive lag means the hard band lags. Error bars on the turnover are 16–84 % from 200 Monte Carlo draws of the lag errors.

Lag spectra use pairs of merged map cells (0.18 dex) and are shown from 0.3 to 32 Hz. The lag axis uses a signed log scale (linear within ±0.1 ms).
